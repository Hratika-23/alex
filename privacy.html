Privacy policy

Alex, personal assistant · last updated 19 November 2026

Who uses Alex

Alex is used only by its owner, Hratika Saxena. No one else can sign in or use it.

What Alex accesses

Only the owner's own Google accounts that she has connected herself: Gmail (to read, label, draft and, with her approval, send or move messages to Bin) and Google Drive (to collect files she uploads for her own projects).

How the data is used

Solely to help the owner manage her own email, files, reminders and expenses. Nothing is used for advertising, profiling or training AI models, and nothing is sold or shared with anyone.

Where the data is kept

On the owner's own private server. Access keys are stored in a locked folder on that server. Content may be processed by the AI service that powers Alex (Anthropic's Claude) to answer the owner's requests.

Google user data

Alex's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Removing access

The owner can remove Alex's access at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions. Stored keys are then deleted from the server.
